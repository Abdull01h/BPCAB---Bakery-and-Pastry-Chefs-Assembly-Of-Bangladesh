Elevating Bangladesh's Baking & Pastry Artistry

Since 2019, BPCAB is the apex professional body uniting executive five-star pastry chefs, artisan sourdough bakers, cafe entrepreneurs, and home baking pioneers across Bangladesh.

President's Message

“Baking is not merely a profession; it is precision, science, and a national identity.”

In 2019, we laid the foundation of BPCAB with a vision to elevate Bangladesh's baking professionals to global international standards. Today we stand united for quality, hygiene, and pastry excellence.

Chef Kazi Mamunur Rashid · President, BPCAB

Our Heritage & Mission

Our Vision

To position Bangladesh as a recognized hub of premium culinary artistry and bakery science, empowering bakers with international certifications and certified hygiene practices.

Our Mission

Foster brotherhood and collaboration among executive chefs, artisan bakers, and passionate home bakers through hands-on masterclasses and supply access.
